Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how GenLeads ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit genleadsagency.com, book an audit, contact us, or interact with automation systems we operate for our clients.

1. Who we are

GenLeads is an AI automation agency based in Kallakurichi, Tamil Nadu 606202, India. For any privacy question, contact shamir@genleadsagency.com or WhatsApp +91 86674 80588.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, business name, website or social profile, and answers you provide when booking an audit or contacting us.
  • Usage information: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location and referral source, collected through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Communications: messages you send us on WhatsApp, email or other channels.

3. How we use it

  • To schedule and prepare your business audit and send confirmations and reminders.
  • To reply to enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To send service-related messages. We only send marketing messages if you agree, and you can opt out at any time.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. WhatsApp and messaging

When you message us or a system we run for a client on WhatsApp, the message is processed through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta, subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. We only send WhatsApp messages to people who have contacted us or agreed to receive them, and you can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

5. Data we process for our clients

When we build and operate automation for a client business, we process their customers' data on that client's behalf and under their instructions. The client is responsible for its own privacy notice to its customers. We use that data only to provide the agreed service.

6. AI processing

Our AI agents use third-party AI model providers to generate replies. Conversation content may be sent to these providers to produce a response. We configure these services so that customer data is not used to train public models where that option is available.

7. Sharing your information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate, such as hosting, calendar, CRM, messaging, payment and AI providers, under appropriate confidentiality and data protection terms, or when required by law.

8. International transfers

Some of our providers store data outside India, including in the United States and the European Union. When data is transferred, we use providers with appropriate safeguards.

9. How long we keep it

We keep audit and enquiry information for up to 24 months after our last contact, and client project data for as long as the service runs plus any period required by law. You can ask us to delete your information earlier.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the UAE's Personal Data Protection Law, you may have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Contact shamir@genleadsagency.com and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the website and, with your consent where required, analytics cookies to understand how visitors use it. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

12. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and reputable providers to protect your information. No system is completely secure, and we will notify affected people and authorities of a data breach where the law requires.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
