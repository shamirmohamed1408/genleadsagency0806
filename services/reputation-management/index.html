Why it matters

Happy customers stay quiet. Unhappy ones post.

Most satisfied customers never think to leave a review, while one frustrated customer can post a 1-star in minutes. New customers check your rating before they call, so a thin or outdated review profile costs you leads every day.

  • Great service, but only a handful of reviews
  • Asking for reviews depends on staff remembering
  • Negative reviews appear before you even hear about the problem
  • Reviews go unanswered for weeks

FAQ

Reputation Management questions.

Is it allowed to ask customers for reviews?

Yes. Google allows you to ask every customer for a review. We ask all customers in the same way and never offer rewards, which keeps you within Google's guidelines.

What happens with unhappy customers?

Customers who rate their experience low are invited to tell you directly first, so you can fix the problem. They can still leave a public review if they choose.

Which review sites does it support?

Google first, plus Facebook, Tripadvisor, Practo, Justdial or any platform that matters in your industry.

Can it reply to reviews?

Yes. The AI drafts a personal reply to every new review for you to approve, or posts replies automatically for 5-star reviews if you prefer.

How long does setup take?

Most review systems go live in 7 to 10 days after the free audit.
