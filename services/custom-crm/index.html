You can't follow up on what you can't see.

Most businesses run on WhatsApp chats, a notebook and a spreadsheet nobody updates. Leads slip through the gaps, follow-ups depend on memory, and nobody knows how much money is in the pipeline.

  • Lead details buried in chats across different phones
  • No reminder when a promising lead goes quiet
  • No clear view of which leads are hot, cold or forgotten
  • Off-the-shelf CRMs so complex that the team stops using them

FAQ

Custom CRM questions.

Is this built from scratch or on an existing CRM?

Whichever fits you best. We can set up and customise a proven CRM, or build a lightweight custom one. Either way it is shaped around your sales process.

Can you move our existing leads and contacts?

Yes. We import your contacts from spreadsheets, phone exports or your current tool as part of the setup.

Can my team use it on their phones?

Yes. It works on desktop and mobile, so your team can update leads from anywhere.

Who owns the data?

You do. Your data stays in accounts you own, and you can export it at any time.

How long does setup take?

Most CRM setups go live in 7 to 10 days after the free audit.
