FAQ

Process Automation questions.

Which payment gateways do you work with?

Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal, UPI payment links and bank transfers. We pick what suits your customers and your country.

Does it work with my accounting software?

Yes. We connect to Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Tally exports or Google Sheets so every paid invoice is recorded automatically.

Are invoices GST and VAT compliant?

Yes. Invoices include your tax details, tax breakdown and numbering, set up for GST in India or VAT in the UAE.

What if a customer pays outside the link?

Your team can mark it paid in one click, or upload the payment screenshot, and the receipt and records update automatically.

How long does setup take?

Most payment automation setups go live in 7 to 10 days after the free audit.
