Why it matters

The first business to reply usually wins.

A lead that messages you is ready to talk right now. Ten minutes later, they have messaged three other businesses. An hour later, they have forgotten why they asked.

  • Enquiries arrive at night, on weekends and while you are with a customer
  • Staff reply when they can, often hours later
  • Leads from Instagram, WhatsApp and email end up in different places
  • Nobody follows up when a lead goes quiet

FAQ

Speed to Lead questions.

Will customers know they are talking to AI?

The agent replies in your tone and can say it is an assistant if you prefer. Whenever a customer asks for a person, or the question needs one, it hands the chat to your team with the full conversation.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Yes, for automated replies. We set up the official WhatsApp Business API on your number as part of the build, so you keep your number.

What if the AI does not know the answer?

It only answers from the information you approve. If it is unsure, it collects the lead's details and alerts your team instead of guessing.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Tamil, Arabic and most major languages. It replies in the language the customer writes in.

How long does setup take?

Most Speed to Lead systems go live in 7 to 10 days after the free audit.
