FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Everything business owners usually ask us before their free audit. Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

What does GenLeads do?

We build AI automation for businesses: instant replies to every lead, a custom CRM, invoice and payment automation, review collection, and websites with all of it connected.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any business that gets enquiries from customers: real estate, property management, hospitality, clinics, salons, software companies, restaurants, education, automotive, professional services and more.

Do you work outside India?

Yes. We work with businesses across India, the UAE and other countries. Everything is set up remotely.

Will it replace my staff?

No. It takes repetitive work off their plate so they can focus on customers and work that needs a person.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so pricing depends on what we build. You get a clear, fixed quote after the free audit, with no surprises.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The 30-minute audit is free, with no obligation. You keep the plan whether or not you work with us.

How long until it is live?

Most systems go live in 7 to 10 days after the audit. Websites take 2 to 3 weeks.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We set everything up, train your team, and handle the technical side.

Will customers know they are talking to AI?

The agent replies in your tone and can say it is an assistant if you prefer. It hands over to a person whenever needed.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Yes, for automated replies. We set it up on your existing number as part of the build.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It only answers from information you approve. If unsure, it collects details and alerts your team instead of guessing.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Tamil, Arabic and most major languages. It replies in the language the customer writes in.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data stays in accounts you own, and we only use the access needed to run your system.

Who owns the accounts and automations?

You do. Everything is set up in your name, with no lock-in.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Ongoing support is month to month. If you stop, your accounts and data stay with you.
